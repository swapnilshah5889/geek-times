# Einstein special relativity — fact-check draft

> **Draft — owner review required.** Claims below were checked against the accessible English translation, not reconciled word-for-word with the 1905 German original. Do not approve or publish from this draft alone.

## Source record

- Albert Einstein, “Zur Elektrodynamik bewegter Körper,” *Annalen der Physik* 17 (1905), 891–921.
- Original-paper DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1002/andp.19053221004>.
- Readable text: [Fourmilab English translation](https://fourmilab.ch/etexts/einstein/specrel/www/), based on the 1923 English edition. Its notes flag changed notation and translation/editorial errors. The section fragments below are present in the HTML: `SECTION11`–`SECTION15`.
- Wiley lists a 2005 republication record separately: <https://doi.org/10.1002/andp.2005517S113>. Use it as a record of the paper identity, not as the original issue pagination.
- Trust: `journal_published`. Historical accounts report that the 1905 Annalen papers were accepted without external peer review ([historical review of peer-review practice](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/07373937.2016.1234347)).
- Claim kinds: all proposed facts are `model_design`; the paper is a theoretical derivation, not an experiment report.

The simultaneity conclusion (§1–2), transformation equations (§3), and velocity-composition rule (§5) were checked as candidates but are not included in this story draft. The velocity formula remains withheld because the 1923 translation flags an equation error.

## Known exclusions

- Do not attribute the famous train-and-lightning thought experiment to this paper.
- Do not attribute `E=mc²` to this paper; Einstein’s 1905 mass–energy note is a separate publication.
- Do not present the paper’s derivations as measured results or claim this paper gives a theory of gravity.
