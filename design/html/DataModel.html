How to read this
Cream publishing · Blue-grey sources & graph · Yellow evidence & media · Green discovery
A Story (red border) is our explainer, stored as one typed document in PublishedVersion. Moments, facts and tags are pulled out of it into tables.
A Source (teal border) is the original work; Relation links sources, so the graph also holds works not explained yet.
Every claim has a kind (illustration / model design / measured result) and every source a trust level.
