Evidence from the paper   /   Real numbers, with sources
  • 6 + 6

    The reader has 6 floors and the writer has 6 floors. The paper calls them encoder and decoder layers.

    Paper §3.1 ↗
  • 8

    lookers work side by side in every attention step. The paper calls them attention heads.

    Paper §3.2.2 ↗
  • 512

    numbers make up one word’s code in the base model. The grid above has exactly 512 squares. Paper name: dmodel.

    Paper §3.1 ↗
  • sin + cos

    Seat numbers are made from smooth sine and cosine waves, then added to each word’s code.

    Paper §3.5 ↗
  • 12 hours

    to train the base model on one computer with 8 NVIDIA P100 graphics chips (100,000 steps). The big model took 3.5 days.
